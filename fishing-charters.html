⚡ My Recommendation

Call the Grand Slam first. It's the largest sportfishing boat on the North Shore, only 38 min from Laie (vs 1hr+ to Honolulu), and built for groups your size. The 8hr or 12hr trip gives you the best shot at landing a big yellowfin. They're the move if availability and price work out.

Best value with confirmed pricing: Whipsaw in Honolulu at $1,699 for a full day (8hr) = ~$283/person. Fits 6, luxury boat, A/C, great reviews. The drive is longer but you know exactly what you're paying.

For the serious tuna hunt: Mattie (47' Cabo with tuna tower) via Sportfish Hawaii at $1,850 for 9 hours. Purpose-built tuna boat, lunch included, and a tuna tower is a real advantage for spotting fish.

⚠️ It's Wednesday — Fri/Sat/Sun slots fill fast. I'd call today to lock something in.

💡 Tips: Bring Dramamine (it gets choppy), reef-safe sunscreen, food/drinks. Budget ~20% tip for the crew. You'll need a Hawaii fishing license ($5 online at fishing.hawaii.gov).
