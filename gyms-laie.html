⚡ My Take

Honest reality: there's no perfect option within 30 min of Laie. Kaneohe gyms (Anytime Fitness, Planet Fitness) are ~48 min away. Haleiwa is ~38 min. The only gyms within 30 min are CrossFit-focused and over budget.

Best bet: North Shore Fitness Club (Laie). Yes it's over budget at $148/mo, but it's walking distance, has barbells and open gym hours. For a month in Hawaii, the time you save not driving 48 min each way might be worth the extra cost. Call and ask about a monthly-only rate — they may cut a deal.

Budget pick: Planet Fitness Kaneohe ($15/mo) if you're okay with a 48-min drive and Smith machines instead of free barbells. Good for dumbbells up to 75 lbs.

Worth a call: CrossFit Koolau (Kahuku, 8 min). Ask if they have open gym hours and what the monthly rate is. If they let you lift outside of class times, it could be the move.

North Shore is very sparse on gyms. You're choosing between close + expensive or cheap + long drive. No way around it.
