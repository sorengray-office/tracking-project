⚡ My Take

Go with Anytime Fitness Kaneohe. It's the only gym within 30 min that checks all your boxes: real free weights (barbell racks, full dumbbell sets), open gym format, 24/7 access, and under $100/mo at ~$65. The 25-min drive is the tradeoff, but there's nothing closer with proper lifting equipment at your price point.

Runner-up: Planet Fitness at $15/mo if you just need something basic while you're here — but the dumbbell cap at 75 lbs and no free barbell racks will limit you if you're doing serious compound lifts.

Worth a call: Three Palms Fitness in Haleiwa — if their price is right and they have good equipment, it's a solid local option at the same ~25 min distance.

Note: North Shore is sparse on gyms. The closest real options all involve a ~25 min drive toward Kaneohe or Haleiwa. The two Laie-area gyms (North Shore Fitness Club, CrossFit Koolau) are CrossFit-focused and over budget.
